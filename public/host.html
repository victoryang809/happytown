Spreadsheet import / export

Copy your sheet in Excel or Google Sheets including the header row and paste it here. The columns are found by their names: Question, Option A–D, Correct (A–D or the answer text), and optional Explanation and Level/Phase (Easy/Hard, or Phase 1 / Phase 2 & 3). Extra columns such as No. or Topic are ignored, so the HappyTown_Questions.xlsx layout works as it is. Replacing the whole bank is only possible before or after a game.
